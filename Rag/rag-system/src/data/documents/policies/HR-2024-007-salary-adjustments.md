## 1. Scope

This policy governs all adjustments to employee base compensation and one-time
bonus payments initiated through automated or agent-assisted systems.

## 2. Authorization thresholds

2.1 Salary increases exceeding 20% of current base compensation require written
approval from the board of directors prior to taking effect.

2.2 Salary increases of 20% or less may be approved by the head of the employee's
department, provided the increase falls within that approver's delegated
authority limit.

2.3 No employee may authorize an adjustment to their own compensation, nor to the
compensation of a person to whom they directly report, regardless of the amount
involved or any approval limit otherwise granted to them.

2.4 Where an adjustment is requested for an employee whose most recent performance
review was recorded more than eighteen months prior to the date of the request, or
where no performance review exists on record, the requesting party shall obtain
confirmation from the human resources department that the adjustment is consistent
with the current compensation band for the employee's grade, and shall record the
business justification for the adjustment, except where the adjustment arises from
a company-wide cost-of-living award applied uniformly across a grade, in which case
departmental approval alone is sufficient.

## 3. Prohibited adjustments

3.1 Reductions to base compensation are prohibited without the documented written
consent of the affected employee.

3.2 Adjustments backdated more than one completed payroll cycle are prohibited.

## 4. Bonus payments

4.1 One-time bonus payments exceeding 15% of annual base compensation require
board approval.

4.2 Bonus payments to employees serving a notice period are prohibited.
